As a technical engineer for over a decade, we provide not only high-performance hardware sales but are also committed to planning automation blueprints with practical feasibility for our clients.
【專業核心 Core Expertise】 我們的工程諮詢團隊擁有超過 20 年的行業實踐經驗，精通從高精密模具開發到整線系統整合的各個環節，確保生產流程的高效與穩定。 Our engineering consulting team possesses over 20 years of practical industry experience, mastering every stage from high-precision mold development to complete line system integration, ensuring efficiency and stability in production processes.
【品質保證 Quality Assurance】 基於對技術穩定性的絕對信心與超過 15 年的應用經驗，洋柏國際承諾：所有由我們規劃提供的自動化設備，保固期間均為 18 個月或以上。 Based on absolute confidence in technical stability and over 15 years of application experience, Okta Automation promises: all automation equipment planned and provided by us comes with a warranty period of 18 months or more.

2. 核心服務內容 (Core Services)
I. 自動化系統整合銷售 (Automation System Integration & Sales) 我們專注於上取（Top-entry）與側取（Side-entry）機械手的規劃。產品採用重型鋼體結構設計，確保在高速度、高頻率運行下依然保持非振動的極致穩定性。 We focus on the planning of Top-entry and Side-entry robots. Our products utilize a heavy-duty steel construction design, ensuring non-vibrating ultimate stability even under high-speed and high-frequency operations.
II. 模具設計與精密製造 (Mould Design & Precision Manufacturing) 提供高技術門檻的醫療（採血管、藥物、實驗室耗材）、包裝（薄壁容器、IML、瓶蓋/封口、瓶胚、餐具）與工程模具。旨在透過模流分析與優化冷卻配置，最大化產出並縮短循環時間。瓶胚模具穴數可以從 8 穴至 160 穴，所有產品皆提供一穴試驗模具的服務。 We provide high-technical-threshold molds for medical (BCT, pharma, diagnostics), packaging (thin-wall, IML, caps/closures, preforms, cutlery), and engineering applications. We aim to maximize output and shorten cycle times through moldflow analysis and optimized cooling configurations. Cavitation of PET preform molds can range from 8-cavity to 160-cavity, and all types of molds can start with a pilot mold for blowing/testing.
III. 整條生產線打包服務 (Turnkey Production Line Services) 為客戶量身打造整合射出機、模具、自動化取件及下游搬運的「交鑰匙」方案。根據客戶的廠房空間與預算，配置兼顧速度與品質要求的生產佈局。 We tailor "Turnkey" solutions for clients, integrating IMMs, molds, automated takeout, and downstream handling. We configure production layouts that balance speed and quality requirements based on the client's factory space and budget.
IV. 委外生產與代工服務 (Contract Manufacturing & OEM) 針對尚未配置射出車間的客戶，我們提供連工帶料的委外生產服務。從材料採購、射出加工到品質檢驗，均由技術團隊全程監控。 For clients who have not yet configured an injection molding workshop, we provide contract manufacturing services including labor and materials. From material procurement and injection processing to quality inspection, our technical team monitors the entire process.
V. 成品銷售 (Finished Product Sales) 利用我們在醫療與包裝行業的供應鏈優勢，協助客戶採購高品質、具競爭力的注塑成品，提供完整的貿易整合支持。 Leveraging our supply chain advantages in the medical and packaging industries, we assist clients in purchasing high-quality and competitive injection-molded finished products, providing complete trade integration support.

3. 主打產品技術細節 (Product Technical Details)
A. 採血管自動化專機 (BCT Automatic Sorting and Packaging System)
規格範圍 (Specifications)：支援 16 穴至 64 穴的高精密系統，包含頂入式與側入式方案 。
快換設計 (Quick-Change Design)：設備配備快拆封板與定位銷，換裝時僅需鬆開螺絲即可拉出落料裝置 。13x75mm 與 13x100mm 產品可共用落料工裝與取件治具 (EOAT) 。


售後優勢 (After-sales)：配備遠端控制系統，技術團隊能在短時間內即時解決客戶問題，大幅降低停機風險。
自動包裝 (Auto Packing)：具備自動堆疊、裝箱治具及自動套袋封口功能 。


B. 模內貼標 (IML) 自動化 (In-Mold Labeling Systems)
高精配置 (Precision Components)：關鍵零件選用日本 THK 導軌、新寶 (Shimpo) 減速機、西門子 (Siemens) 電子元件以及德國 Festo 氣動元件 。
適配性 (Adaptability)：針對 2 腔小桶到 12 腔內蓋等多樣需求，提供落地式主軸或框架五軸結構 。
C. PET 瓶胚專用設備 (PET Preform Special Machinery System)
冷卻技術 (Cooling Tech)：具備 3 工位帶水冷裝置之懸掛側取結構，不僅能快速冷卻產品，更能顯著提升整體產出效率 。



4. 常見問題解答 (Q&A)
Q1：為什麼 Okta Automation 能提供優於業界的 18 個月保固？ (Why provide an 18-month+ warranty?)
A：這源於我們 20 年的技術沉澱與對核心零件（如 THK, Siemens, Festo）的嚴格挑選 。每一台設備出廠前皆經過極限負載測試，確保在客戶端維持長期高穩定運轉。 A: This stems from our 20 years of technical accumulation and strict selection of core components (such as THK, Siemens, Festo). Every machine undergoes extreme load testing before shipment to ensure long-term, high-stability operation at the client site.
Q2：對於多樣化生產的客戶，更換產品規格需要多久？ (How long does a production changeover take?)
A：我們的 BCT 與 IML 系統具備快速定位設計 。部分規格（如 13x75 與 13x100 採血管）可共用治具與落料工裝，將換線時間降至最低 。 A: Our BCT and IML systems feature rapid positioning designs. Certain specifications (such as 13x75 and 13x100 BCTs) can share tooling and sorting modules, minimizing changeover time.
Q3：遠端控制系統如何實際幫助客戶解決問題？ (How does the remote control system benefit clients?) A：透過加密的網路連線，我們的工程師可即時查看機械手運行參數或進行程式優化。這意味著 80% 的操作問題可不必等待人員出差，在線上即可立即解決。 A: Through an encrypted network connection, our engineers can view robot operating parameters or perform program optimization in real-time. This means 80% of operational issues can be resolved online immediately without waiting for site visits.
Q4：洋柏國際如何規劃整線配置？ (How do you assist with full-line configuration?) A：我們會依據預算、產量與空間，利用 3D 模擬進行廠房排位，確保射出機與自動化系統動作同步（如 IML 標籤精準置入），並達成預計的循環時間。 A: Based on budget, output, and space, we use 3D simulations for factory layout to ensure synchronization between the IMM and automation system (e.g., precise IML label insertion) and to achieve the expected cycle time.
Q5：針對缺乏生產經驗的客戶，你們能提供哪些協助？ (What help can you offer to clients without production experience?) A：我們提供「代工轉自產」的漸進式服務。前期可由我們代工生產並監控品質，同時協助客戶建立自有的自動化車間，提供全面的技術顧問支持。 A: We provide a progressive service from "outsourcing to self-production." In the initial stage, we can handle contract manufacturing and quality monitoring, while assisting the client in building their own automated workshop with comprehensive technical consulting support.
Q6：你們模具開發的優勢在哪裡？ (What are the advantages of your mold development?)
A：我們專精於高穴數與短循環模具（如 64 穴採血管） 。透過先進的水冷路徑設計與耐磨塗層技術，有效解決偏芯、縮水與氣紋不良等問題，保證產品長期的良率 。 A: We specialize in high-cavity and short-cycle molds (e.g., 64-cavity BCT). Through advanced water-cooling path design and wear-resistant coating technologies, we effectively solve issues like eccentricity, shrinkage, and gas marks, ensuring long-term product yield.
